# Current 1.4.0

- **Record a voiceover.** Press R, or the microphone button above the timeline. Pick your microphone and check its level, then set a countdown: 3 seconds unless you change it, or 0 to start at once. Recording starts at the playhead while the video plays, and the take goes on a free audio track. A small panel shows the time, the level and Stop recording, and you can drag it anywhere so the preview stays in view. Space also stops. Recordings are saved in `~/Videos/Current voiceovers`.
- **Ten professional intros, each with a matching outro**, for lessons and company videos: Block Reveal, Lesson Opener, Side Panel, Line Frame, Bold Bands, Lesson Agenda, Chart Rise, Chapter Number, Monogram and Shutter. They are first in the Intro/Outro tab, and also under Titles › Professional. Every shape in them, not just the text, can be dragged on its own.
- **Professional lower thirds**: a name-and-role lower third in each of the ten professional styles, under Titles › Professional.
- **Record your screen** with an optional round webcam bubble and your microphone on its own track (Shift+R). Your desktop picks the screen or window; Current can hide itself while you record.
- **Automatic captions**, made on this computer with whisper.cpp (installed with Current), in 17 languages. Each caption appears as its words are spoken and leaves during pauses. Edit every line, pick any subtitle style (Word Highlight follows the real timing of each word), and show them on screen, as an .srt file next to the exported video, or both.
- **Brand kits**: save your colours, heading font, logo and company name. Keep several, choose the one in use from the Brand menu, or switch branding off. New titles, stickers and intros take the kit's look.
- **Auto-ducking**: music dips under voices by itself. Mark any clip as Voice, Music or Sound effect.
- **Remove pauses** cuts the quiet stretches out of a clip and closes the gaps.
- **Zoom to area**: drag a box over the preview to zoom smoothly into part of a clip, and back out.
- **Shapes**: show any video or photo as a rounded rectangle or a circle, with an outline.
- **Effects update right away.** Adding or removing an effect now shows in the Effects tab immediately, without switching tabs.
- **Track names line up with their tracks.** The names on the left of the timeline sat half a row too high.

Install:

```bash
sudo apt install ./current_1.4.0_amd64.deb
```
