# Current 1.4.1

Everything new in 1.4 is now easy to find.

- **New Clip menu.** Everything you can do to the selected clip, in one place: split, freeze frame, detach audio, crop, speed, color, green screen, zoom to area, shape and outline, picture in picture, audio settings, remove pauses and captions for that clip. Items that need a clip are greyed out until you select one.
- **New Tools menu** for recording a voiceover or your screen, generating and editing captions, and choosing whether captions show on screen, as a caption file, or both. The Edit menu is back to just editing.
- **Right-click a clip** for the same tools, showing only what applies to that clip.
- **Labelled buttons** above the timeline for Voiceover, Screen and Captions. The other buttons are grouped by what they do and stay greyed out until you select a clip they work on.
- **Menus open the right spot.** Choosing Zoom to area, Shape and outline, Remove pauses or Clip type opens that part of the clip panel and highlights it. The clip panel now starts at the top whenever you select another clip or tab.
- **Help › What’s new** lists every 1.4 feature, where to find it, and a button to open it. It is also on the start screen.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** are grouped into Playback, Editing, Timeline, Recording, and Files and panels.
- **View lists every library tab**, Intro/Outro included, in sidebar order. Alt+1 to Alt+8 now match the sidebar.
- **Clearer timeline ruler.** Close up, the ruler shows seconds such as 00:01.5 instead of frame counts that looked like minutes.
- **New icons** for freeze frame (a frame with pause bars) and brand kits (colour swatches).

Install:

```bash
sudo apt install ./current_1.4.1_amd64.deb
```
